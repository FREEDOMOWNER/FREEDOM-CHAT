Private by Design
No email required, no personal data collected, no tracking. Your identity is known only by the username you choose.
End-to-End Encrypted
All communications are encrypted via the Tor network. Your conversations cannot be intercepted or monitored.
Anonymous Access
Fully compatible with Tor Browser. The .onion address provides authentication and encryption without certificates.
Messages Self-Destruct
All messages are automatically deleted 24 hours after posting. Nothing is stored forever.
16-Word Recovery
Your account is secured by a recovery phrase — no email resets, no backdoors, no third-party dependency.
Community Moderated
Dedicated moderators keep the platform safe while respecting your privacy and freedom.
